    How Lattice handles your data

    Please read this before you create the project.

    • Stored in this browser. Projects, sources (including their full text), and cards live in this browser’s storage. There is no Lattice account or cloud database.
    • Sent to OpenAI for card extraction. When AI is set up, the text of each source and piece of your own work you add, plus this project’s question and hypothesis, is sent to OpenAI to create evidence cards (up to about 60,000 characters per item).
    • Sent to OpenAI to connect ideas. Each card’s claim (and the project’s question) is sent to OpenAI to find how your ideas relate: which support, contradict, refine, repeat, or explain each other.
    • Links are fetched by your local server. Lattice downloads the pages and PDFs you link to.
    • You stay in control. Back up or delete everything at any time from Privacy & data.

    Delete this project

    Removes the project, its sources, cards, and activity from this browser. This can’t be undone unless you have a backup.

        From a pile of reading
        to a map of your thinking.

        What happens to a source from the moment you add it, where your data goes, and how Lattice decides which ideas are connected.

        1 · THE JOURNEY OF A SOURCE

        Six steps from a paper to your web

        1. 1＋

          Add evidence

          A link, arXiv paper, DOI, PDF, pasted text, or a passage captured in Chrome. You say whether it's an external source, your experiment, or your draft.

          On your device
        2. 2▤

          Read the text

          PDFs and pasted text are read in your browser. Links are downloaded by Lattice's local server. The full text is stored in your browser.

          On your device
        3. 3✦

          Find the main conclusions

          The text (up to about 60,000 characters) and your question and hypothesis go to OpenAI, which returns a few main conclusions, each with supporting quotes.

          Sent to OpenAI
        4. 4✓

          Check every quote

          Each quote is checked word for word against the source text. Anything the AI paraphrased or invented is dropped before you see it.

          On your device
        5. 5⇆

          You review

          New cards appear in a deck, one at a time. You accept or reject each one. Only accepted cards become part of your project.

          On your device
        6. 6◎

          Connect the ideas

          Accepted claims are compared with your hypothesis and with each other, and the AI decides how they relate. The result is your idea web.

        3 · HOW CONNECTIONS ARE DECIDED

        Lattice compares only what's worth comparing

        “Copper speeds up vitamin C loss”

        Each accepted claim is turned into a list of numbers (an embedding) that captures its meaning.

        →
        Every hypothesis guess + 3 closest ideas from other sources

        Each idea is paired with every guess, and with the few ideas from other sources that mean the most similar thing.

        →
        AI reads each pair

        The model decides how the two claims relate, how sure it is, and gives a one-line reason. It judges only the two claims, with no outside knowledge.

        →
        Stored once

        Verdicts are saved, so a reload costs nothing. Editing a claim only re-checks that claim's pairs.

        Supports one claim is evidence for the other Contradicts they can't both be true Refines adds a condition or a limit Same claim says the same thing Explains gives a cause or mechanism

        • Dark cards are the guesses your hypothesis was split into. Each one counts the evidence for and against it.
