# Lattice launch checklist

## What is ready in this workspace

- Local research projects, capture, evidence classification, and Cards library.
- Local-first privacy controls, backup/restore, and delete flow.
- Chrome-extension capture scaffold.
- [Landing page](../landing.html) with positioning and an initial beta offer.

## Do before inviting external beta users

1. Test installation and capture on a clean Chrome profile and on at least two machines.
2. Replace the local static server with a signed desktop build or an installable hosted app.
3. Publish actual Privacy Policy and Terms pages; do not use the landing page as a substitute.
4. Add a support email and a monitored bug-report channel.
5. Decide whether the beta is invite-only and establish a consented feedback process.
6. Recruit 10–20 people with an active research project. Watch them complete: source → evidence classification → next action → return visit.

## Measure the beta

- Activation: researcher adds a source, makes one evidence judgment, and saves one next action.
- Weekly value: researcher returns to an existing project.
- Trust: the user can find the source passage behind every retained card.
- Capture value: a meaningful share of browser captures become a classified passage.

## Delay until beta demonstrates retention

- Payments and subscription infrastructure.
- Shared/team workspaces.
- Cloud synchronization.
- Autonomous literature search or claims of research novelty.

## External launch actions that still need a human decision

- Set a real price and connect a payment provider.
- Choose a legal entity, a support channel, and a publishing/deployment host.
- Review legal language for the intended market.
- Approve and publish the final public landing page and product policies.
