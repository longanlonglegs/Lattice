  3. 3

    Make a judgment

    See support, contradictions, questions, and local next actions—without claims about the literature that your sources cannot support.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Your unpublished work is not the product.

Lattice stores research projects in your browser’s local storage. Your selected browser capture goes only to your open local Lattice tab. Anki is contacted only when you choose export.

Back up, restore, or delete your complete local workspace from the Privacy & data view.
