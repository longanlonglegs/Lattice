# Surface brief: Lattice app (index.html) and landing page (landing.html)

Scope: whole-app visual redesign plus landing page. Mode: Operate (app); Persuade (landing.html).
Audience: students and newcomers to research; seen mostly projected on a big screen to judges. Must not feel playful or childish. Keep: the name Lattice; the web's layout and behaviour (hypothesis in the middle, evidence gathered around it, drag, pan, zoom).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lattice is a canvas-first research tool at the craft level of Figma, Miro and Obsidian's graph view: the idea web is a full-bleed infinite canvas with floating tool chrome, and every other tab is a calm, dense panel in the same chrome. Refuses the warm paper, serif, terracotta "academic notebook" look it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral light-grey app chrome (#f5f5f5 canvas, white panels, hairline #e6e6e6 borders), one UI typeface (Inter) at projector-friendly sizes with a mono for codes and counts, a single blue reserved for selection, focus, and primary actions, and saturated origin and relation colours that only ever mean origin or relation. Floating panels have 12px corners and a soft two-layer shadow; dark charcoal hub cards are the hypothesis.

STORY: The visitor sees their hypothesis at the centre of a canvas, evidence gathering around it with coloured lines, and understands within seconds that every claim is traced to a quote; they review, explore, and read Insights without leaving that chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 48px top bar (logo, project switcher, breadcrumbs, share-style actions on the right: Add evidence as the one blue primary). Below it the Web tab fills the window edge to edge: dotted canvas, a floating left panel (search, filters as a layer list, legend), a floating bottom-centre toolbar (fit, sticky note, show all connections, needs attention), zoom controls bottom-right, and a right inspector panel that opens when a card is selected. Tabs sit as a segmented control in the top bar.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen by the user after two direction rounds; reference products Figma, Miro, Obsidian graph. Seed key b84820a3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
